## What We Collect

We collect the following information through the participant questionnaire:

### Identity & Contact
- Nickname (display name)
- Email address

### Questionnaire Responses
- Position on the E-Soviets proposal (6-option scale)
- Motivation interests (up to 5 selections)
- Weekly volunteer availability (8-option scale)
- Skills (up to 15 selections across 5 categories)
- Experience level (6-option scale)
- Portfolio URL (optional)
- Preferred Soviet (8 options)
- Secondary Soviets (up to 3)
- Delegate interest (5-option scale)
- Delegate motivations (if interested, up to 9 selections)
- Practical project proposal (50-2500 characters)
- AI/Robotics proposal (max 2500 characters)
- First month actions (multiple selections)
- Participation modes (multiple selections)
- Consent confirmations (contact, accuracy, public profile)

### Technical Data
- User agent (browser/OS info)
- Submission timestamp (UTC)
- Unique participant ID (`ES-2026-XXXXXX`)
- Submission ID (UUID)

---

## Why We Collect It

| Data | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Nickname, Email | Contact participants, send participant card, organize communication |
| Proposal Position | Understand alignment with E-Soviets proposal |
| Motivation Interests | Match participants to relevant work areas |
| Availability | Plan project capacity and timelines |
| Skills | Match participants to Soviet needs and projects |
| Experience Level | Gauge expertise for task assignment |
| Portfolio URL | Verify skills and experience (optional) |
| Soviet Preferences | Assign participants to relevant Soviets |
| Delegate Interest | Identify potential representatives |
| Proposals | Collect project ideas for first-year planning |
| Participation Modes | Coordinate communication preferences |
| Consent | Legal basis for processing and storage |

---

## Who Can Access It

- **E-Soviets organizers** with access to the Google Sheet
- **System administrators** maintaining the infrastructure
- **No third parties** - data is not shared, sold, or transferred

Access is limited to individuals explicitly granted permission on the Google Sheet by the project organizers.

---

## Data Retention

**The retention period will be determined by the E-Soviets organizers before launch and documented here.**

This application does **not** hardcode a retention period. The organizers must decide:

- How long to keep responses after submission
- Whether to archive or delete after a period
- Process for data deletion upon participant request

**Until a retention policy is set, data will be retained indefinitely** in the Google Sheet. Participants can request deletion at any time (see below).

---

## Your Rights

You can request:

1. **Access** - Copy of your data
2. **Correction** - Fix inaccurate information
3. **Deletion** - Remove your data from our records
4. **Portability** - Export your data in a standard format

To exercise these rights, contact: **privacy@e-soviets.org** (to be configured)

We will respond within 30 days.

---

## Security

- Data transmitted over HTTPS
- Stored in Google Sheets (Google's security)
- Service account with minimal permissions (Editor on one sheet only)
- No credentials in client-side code
- Rate limiting and honeypot anti-spam

## Cookies & Tracking

This application uses **no cookies**, **no tracking pixels**, **no analytics**, and **no third-party scripts**.
